About Akij City Center

Akij City Center is more than just a commercial complex—it's a lifestyle destination where people come together to shop, dine, work, and celebrate life.

OUR STORY

Built on Legacy.
Driven by Purpose.

Rooted in the values and legacy of Akij Group, Akij City Center was established to create a world-class commercial and lifestyle destination in the southern region of Bangladesh.

From modern retail to family entertainment, from quality services to community spaces—our journey continues with a promise to enrich lives and build a better tomorrow.

THE LEGACY BEHIND AKIJ
Sk. Akij Uddin (1929–2006)
Founder of Akij Group

From humble beginnings to building one of Bangladesh's leading business groups, Akij Uddin's visionary leadership continues to inspire the Akij legacy.In 1950, Sk. Akij Uddin started his journey as a modest trader. Through sheer grit, visionary enterprise, and unyielding ethics, he transformed a small venture into one of Bangladesh’s most trusted industrial giants. Today, his principles remain our guiding compass.
