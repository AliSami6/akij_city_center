FOUNDER OF AKIJ GROUP

THE PARAGON OF
VISIONARY LEADERS

Born in 1929 in Madhyadanga, Phultala, Khulna, Bangladesh, Mr. Akij Uddin was a visionary whose journey in business began in 1950 as a fruit vendor and trader.

With his revolutionary vision and leadership skills, he built an empire with multiple successful businesses ranging from jute, cigarettes, groceries, printing & packaging, textiles, hardboards, leather processing, pharmaceuticals, to real estate. The highly respected and revered entrepreneur left this world in 2006, but his legacy lives on.

Sk. Akij Uddin (1929–2006)
Founder of Akij Group

Let's Explore The Golden Legacy Remarkable Journey Glorious History of Akij Group.

Driven by quality and trust, Akij Group has been shaping industries and serving millions across the globe for over seven decades with excellence and integrity.

THE BEGINNING
1950

Humble Beginning

Humble beginning by Mr. Sk. Akij Uddin, starting his business journey as a fruit vendor.

1952

Bidi & Jute Expansion

Inception of handmade cigarette business, expanding into jute trading and early manufacturing units.

1974

Industrial Growth

Rapid industrial expansion with the establishment of 22 different business concerns across multiple sectors.

1980

Ad-Din Trust & CSR

Founding of Ad-Din Welfare Trust for CSR, becoming the highest tax-paying group.

2006

Diversification

Modernization era with diversification into cement, ceramics, textiles, food & beverage industries.

2015

Global Reach

Transformation into a global industrial giant managing 45+ SBUs with export presence in 40+ countries.
