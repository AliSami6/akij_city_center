OUR PURPOSE
OUR MISSION

To build a world-class business ecosystem that fosters innovation, growth, and sustainability. We provide cutting-edge infrastructure and facilities that empower businesses of all sizes to thrive and contribute to the economic development of Bangladesh.

OUR VISION

To become the leading destination where commerce, lifestyle and community come together, driving economic prosperity and global connectivity. We aim to empower businesses, create jobs, and contribute to a stronger, more competitive nation.

THE LEGACY BEHIND AKIJ
Sk. Akij Uddin (1929–2006)
Founder of Akij Group

From humble beginnings to building one of Bangladesh's leading business groups, Akij Uddin's visionary leadership continues to inspire the Akij legacy.In 1950, Sk. Akij Uddin started his journey as a modest trader. Through sheer grit, visionary enterprise, and unyielding ethics, he transformed a small venture into one of Bangladesh’s most trusted industrial giants. Today, his principles remain our guiding compass.
