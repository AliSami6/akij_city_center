DISCOVER WHO WE ARE

Founded on the pillars of trust, quality, and progress, Akij Group is one of Bangladesh's most renowned and respected industrial conglomerates. For decades, we have been driving national growth through continuous innovation, sustainable practices, and an unwavering commitment to excellence across diverse sectors.

From manufacturing to consumer goods, our mission remains simple: creating value for our nation, empowering communities, and setting benchmark standards of quality in every endeavor we undertake.

THE ROOTS OF EXCELLENCE

The Paragon of Visionary Leaders

"Born in 1929 in Madhyadanga, Phultala, Khulna, Bangladesh, Mr. Akij Uddin was a visionary whose journey in business began in 1950 as a fruit vendor and trader."

With his revolutionary vision and leadership skills, he built an empire with multiple successful businesses ranging from jute, cigarettes, groceries, printing & packaging, textiles, hardboards, leather processing, pharmaceuticals, to real estate. The highly respected and revered entrepreneur left this world in 2006, but his legacy lives on.

A History To Be Proud Of

Founded by the visionary industrialist Sheikh Akij Uddin in the 1940s, Akij Group has grown from a modest trading setup into one of Bangladesh's largest and most trusted conglomerates. Driven by values of integrity, quality, and social commitment, the group spans diverse sectors including textiles, beverages, jute, tobacco, cement, and consumer goods.

  • Visionary Leadership
  • Industrial Excellence
  • Global Export Reach
  • Socio-Economic Impact
  • Sustainable Growth
1940s

Foundation & Early Beginnings

Sheikh Akij Uddin started his entrepreneurial journey trading jute, paddy, and coconut, laying the cornerstone of what would become the Akij Group.

1960s

Expansion into Jute & Match Operations

Diversified into manufacturing by setting up match factories and expanding jute trading networks, cementing a strong industrial base.

1990s

Textile & Printing Expansion

Launched Akij Textile Mills and state-of-the-art packaging facilities to support rapid industrial growth across national and export markets.

2006

Akij Food & Beverage Ltd.

Entered the FMCG sector with AFBL, producing iconic local beverage brands like Mojo, Lemu, and Speed, rapidly capturing national market share.

2010s

Cement & Ceramics Leadership

Established Akij Cement and Akij Ceramics, revolutionizing building materials in Bangladesh with premium-quality standards.

Present

Diversified Global Conglomerate

Employs over 35,000+ people across 20+ subsidiary companies, leading in innovation, corporate responsibility, and national development.
